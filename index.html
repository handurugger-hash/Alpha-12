Algebra Solver Guide
How to use each tab
📐 Equations
The most flexible tab. Type any equation or expression — linear, quadratic, cubic, or higher — and hit Solve. You can use = to set up an equation like x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 or just paste an expression like 3x^2 - 12. It gives you the expanded form, factored form, and all solutions. Use the example chips to get started fast.
Expanded form Factored form All solutions (real + complex)
🔢 Quadratic
Enter the three coefficients of ax² + bx + c = 0 — just the numbers. Hit Solve and a popup asks exactly what you want to find. Every step of the work is shown so you can follow along.
Roots via quadratic formula (step-by-step) Vertex (h, k) with steps Vertex form a(x−h)²+k Axis of symmetry Parabola direction
📍 Vertex & Form
Type a quadratic expression and hit Analyze. A popup asks what you want. Great when you already have the expression written out and want to convert it or find specific properties without entering a, b, c separately.
Vertex (h, k) Axis of symmetry Vertex form conversion Min or max value
📈 Parabola Builder
Have x,y coordinate points and need the equation of the parabola? Enter at least 3 pairs and hit Find Equation. It fits the best quadratic curve to your points and shows a verification table to check the fit.
Equation f(x) = ax²+bx+c Coefficients a, b, c Vertex and y-intercept Verification table Fit quality indicator
✂️ Intercepts
Type any polynomial and get its x-intercepts (where it crosses the x-axis) and y-intercept (where it crosses the y-axis) as clean coordinate points. x-intercepts are the same as solutions — just presented differently.
y-intercept as a point (0, c) x-intercepts as points (r, 0) Solutions listed Complex roots noted
🧮 Arithmetic
A clean calculator for any math expression. Supports +, −, *, /, exponents (^), square roots (sqrt()), and constants like pi and e. Great for quickly plugging x into a function to check a value.
Exact result Decimal rounded to 6 places
Input Tips
Use ^ for exponents (x^2 = x²)  ·  Multiplication is implied (2x works fine)  ·  Parentheses are always safe to add  ·  Works with variables x, y, t, and more
